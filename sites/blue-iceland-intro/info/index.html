Ice cave tour: what to know before we go

普通话讲解，中英文字幕 · Mandarin with Chinese and English subtitles · 1 min 43 s

行程要点

The key points

  1. 1
    坐乌尼莫克超级吉普车，开车大约 30 分钟到冰川。
    We ride a big Unimog super jeep for about 30 minutes to the glacier.
  2. 2
    在停车场领取头盔和冰爪。冰爪请自己拿到冰洞。
    At the parking lot you get a helmet and crampons. Carry your crampons to the cave.
  3. 3
    步行约 30 分钟，走过冰碛（石头和沙子），中等难度。
    A 30-minute walk over glacier moraine (rocks and sand). Moderately challenging.
  4. 4
    冰洞里有很多别的旅行团，请一直跟着我们的团。
    There are many other groups in the cave. Always stay with your group.
  5. 5
    整个行程中，任何时候都不能摘下头盔。
    Never take your helmet off at any time during the trip.

请穿好

Please wear

  • 有护踝的登山靴 · Hiking boots with ankle support
  • 暖和、防水的衣服，帽子和手套 · Warm, waterproof layers, a hat and gloves
