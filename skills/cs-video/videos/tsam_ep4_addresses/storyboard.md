# TSAM: Addresses (storyboard)

**Learning goal:** after watching, the viewer can read an IPv4 address as 32 bits with a network and host part, use a /24 mask, find a broadcast address, explain CIDR vs classes, MAC vs IP, ARP and DHCP's DORA (incl. rogue DHCP).

**Format:** cartoon cold open (Sam, Ada, sometimes Mallory; `open/`) that poses the problem, then a Manim
explanation (`video.py`) in short chapters, each with its own title and visual change. Details and
calculations are kept light: this is an introduction before working the past exams.

**Hook:** Sam checks in at the network hotel: Ada (DHCP) offers room 100; Mallory runs a rogue desk.
**Your turn:** broadcast address of 10.4.7.0/24 (10.4.7.255).
**Teaser:** 4.3 billion addresses, 20 billion devices: NAT.

Scenes: see `SCENES` in `video.py`; dialogue in `open/lines.py`.
