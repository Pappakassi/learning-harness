# TSAM: Trust on the Internet (storyboard)

**Learning goal:** after watching, the viewer can follow a DNS lookup (recursive vs iterative), explain spoofing, DNSSEC and open resolvers, the key-exchange problem and Diffie-Hellman, certificate authorities, Kerckhoffs' principle, nonces, why not MD5 for passwords, and firewalls.

**Format:** cartoon cold open (Sam, Ada, sometimes Mallory; `open/`) that poses the problem, then a Manim
explanation (`video.py`) in short chapters, each with its own title and visual change. Details and
calculations are kept light: this is an introduction before working the past exams.

**Hook:** Sam's secret note passes through Mallory, who reads and edits it.
**Your turn:** can Mallory mix the shared colour from yellow, orange and green? (no)
**Teaser:** end of the series: try the old exams.

Scenes: see `SCENES` in `video.py`; dialogue in `open/lines.py`.
