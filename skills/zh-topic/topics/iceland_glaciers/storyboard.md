# 冰島的冰川 — video storyboard (Mandarin, ink style, ~3 min)

**Learning goal:** the viewer can explain in Chinese how snow becomes glacier ice, why
old ice is blue, why volcanoes under glaciers cause floods, and why Iceland's glaciers
are shrinking, and knows the core words used in the book (冰川、融化、火山灰、冰川洪水…).

**Format:** *build it up* + *picture first*. Same facts and words as `book.txt`
(sources in `research.md`), so the video primes the vocabulary for the book.

| # | Scene | On screen | Narration (gist) |
|---|-------|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | `Hook` | Real Iceland outline draws itself in ink; glaciers wash in blue; red points pulse under the ice (Grímsvötn, Bárðarbunga, Katla). Title after. | 冰島 has 冰 in its name but only 1/10 is ice. Under the ice hide volcanoes. Ice + fire: what happens? |
| 2 | `SnowToIce` | Snow layers fall year after year; lower layers compress, bubbles squeezed out, colour shifts to blue; the stack shears into a flowing slope. | Snow → 冰粒 → ice; thick enough, it flows under its own weight. |
| 3 | `WhyBlue` | Sunlight enters an ice block as red/green/blue rays; red is absorbed, blue reaches the eye. | Old dense ice absorbs red, blue comes back. |
| 4 | `HowBig` | Vatnajökull's real outline vs a square with Shanghai's area. Then 950 m ice column vs Taipei 101 (508 m); **your turn**: how many 101s? countdown → two. | 7,700 km² > Shanghai 6,341 km²; up to 950 m thick. |
| 5 | `FireUnderIce` | Cross-section: ice cap on rock, magma below; heat melts a pocket; water bursts out toward the sea; a bridge shakes and falls (2011). | Jökulhlaup = 冰川洪水. |
| 6 | `Shrinking` | Map: glaciers shrink while years count 1890→2019; Ok fades out; plaque "給未來的一封信". | −2,200 km² (≈18%); Ok lost in 2014; plaque 2019; ~200 years. |
| 7 | `Teaser` | Brushed ensō; line inviting the viewer to listen to the ice; next story: 冰島的歷史. | |
